# Little messes test checklist

Goal: every so often the buddy (cat, dog or bird) squats on the taskbar and leaves a little pile behind. Click a pile to clean it up.

Get the build the same way as before: download the `DesktopBuddy-win-x64` artifact from the pull request's latest **build** run. Exit the old version first.

The cat goes after 25 to 45 minutes of you using the PC (time away doesn't count). Open **Diagnostics** from the tray menu to see the **Next pile in** countdown and how many piles are on the taskbar.

| # | What to do | What should happen | Pass? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open Settings | There is a new option, **Leave a little mess now and then (click it to clean up)**, ticked | |
| 2 | Keep working until Next pile in reaches 0 | The cat stops, crouches with its tail up and looks around, shuts its eyes for a moment, then walks off. A small brown pile with wavy stink lines is left just behind where it squatted | |
| 3 | Hover over the pile | The pointer is a hand and the tooltip says "Clean it up" | |
| 4 | Click the pile | It vanishes in a puff of dust and sparkles. Sometimes the cat says thanks | |
| 5 | Click the cat while it's squatting | Nothing happens; it finishes first | |
| 6 | Pick the cat up while it's squatting | No pile is left behind | |
| 7 | Leave three piles on the taskbar, then wait for the next one | No fourth pile. A bubble asks for a cleanup instead | |
| 8 | Open a fullscreen app (or Hide from the tray menu) with a pile on the taskbar | The piles hide with the cat and come back with it | |
| 9 | With an auto-hide taskbar, leave a pile and let the taskbar slide away and back | The pile stays sitting on the taskbar edge, like the cat | |
| 10 | Start a focus timer | The cat never squats during a session | |
| 11 | Untick the option in Settings with piles on the taskbar | They puff away, Diagnostics shows Next pile in: off, and no new ones appear | |
| 12 | Repeat 2 to 4 with a couple of the other cats and dogs from the Buddy list, facing both ways | Each has its own squat drawing, and the pile lands under its tail | |
| 13 | Repeat 2 to 4 with a couple of birds | The bird leans forward with its tail flicked up, and leaves a small white splat instead of a pile. Its bubbles chirp ("Cheep! Cleanup, please?") | |
| 14 | Leave a pile as a cat, then switch to a bird in Settings | The pile stays a pile until you click it | |
